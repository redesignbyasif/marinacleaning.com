How it works

From first hello
to your first clean.

No contracts, no pressure. Four steps, most of them happening in the first 48 hours.

  1. 01

    Get in touch

    Call, text or email. We arrange a free walk-through and ask what you actually need — then tell you exactly what to expect from us within 48 hours.

  2. 02

    Two quotes, one email

    A welcome email with a quote for the initial deep clean (hourly, since the first visit takes longer) and a fixed rate for regular visits.

  3. 03

    Your first clean

    We find your place in the schedule. Notes from the walk-through travel with the crew, so nobody arrives guessing.

  4. 04

    Feedback, then rhythm

    After the first visits we ask what to adjust. Negative comments aren't complaints to us — they're the reason the second clean is better than the first.

Good to know

Questions,
answered plainly.

A few of the things clients ask before the first visit. If yours isn't here, the phone is faster than any form.

Call (207) 337-3315
How many cleaners will be in my home?

Usually two. Depending on the scope and the size of the house, we may send three or four.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No contracts. We only ask for 48 hours' notice to cancel or move an appointment — after that, a cancellation fee of about half your regular fee applies.

Who provides the supplies?

We do — all cleaning supplies and equipment come with us. If you prefer a specific product, leave it out and we'll use it.

How do I give access if I'm not home?

Most clients aren't home during the clean. We arrange a secure method with you, usually a duplicate key. If we can't get in on a scheduled day, a fee of half the normal rate applies.

Why is the first cleaning more expensive?

The first visit takes longer — it's a new space for us, and we're setting the standard for your home. We only charge for the extra time it actually needs.

What if something is damaged or missing?

We carry full insurance for accidents, rare as they are. For a missing item, call the office: most turn up within 24 hours, and we'll look into it with you honestly and respectfully.

What if I'm not happy with the clean?

Tell us right away. We'll do what's needed to put it right, including a second cleaning. We can't improve on what we don't hear about.
